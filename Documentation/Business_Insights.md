# Business Insights

## Revenue Concentration

The UK represents the largest share of revenue in the dataset.

## Customer Retention

Repeat customers contribute approximately 93% of total revenue.

## Product Performance

Paper Craft, Little Birdie is the leading product by both units sold and revenue.

## International Markets

Several non-UK markets demonstrate meaningful product demand and can be evaluated for further business expansion.

## Seasonal Performance

Revenue varies considerably across months, with stronger performance around September–November and a decline in December.
